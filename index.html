How this list is built

Earlier runs of this tracker had no way to read a live listing page and inferred status from search-engine text, which produced four confident matches that were all off market. That failure mode is now closed off.

StepWhat happens
EnumerateEvery listing in the three cities is pulled from MetroListPRO’s own city indexes — the MLS of record itself, active and pending alike — 377 properties this run, not a search-result sample.
FilterHard criteria applied to structured MLS fields, never to prose: 29 cleared beds/baths/price, then acreage and pool narrowed it to 7.
VerifyEach survivor is re-read from MetroListPRO, the official MetroList MLS site, and price, beds, full baths, acreage and pool must match. 5 agreed, checked 2026-10-30. 2 listings are carried by a different MLS — 2520 Bedford Ave (MAX), 3538 Wildwood Ln (BAREIS) — so MetroListPRO has no record of them and never will. They are confirmed against the MLSListings.com MLS sheet instead.
ResolveWhere sources disagree, the MLS of record wins and the disagreement is printed on the card rather than hidden.

Two things that bite here and are handled explicitly: half baths — some sites report 2 full + 1 half as “3 baths”, so the bath test counts full baths only; and MetroList MLS number prefixes encode the listing year (226… = 2026), which makes a stale listing easy to spot. Every MetroList-carried property on this page has one; the single exception is 2520 Bedford Ave (MLS 41150059, MAX), numbered by a different MLS, so the year cannot be read off it this way and its card says how it was checked instead.

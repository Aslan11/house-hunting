How this list is built

Earlier runs of this tracker had no way to read a live listing page and inferred status from search-engine text, which produced four confident matches that were all off market. That failure mode is now closed off.

StepWhat happens
EnumerateEvery active listing in the three cities is pulled from an IDX feed carrying MetroList data — 296 properties this run, not a search-result sample.
FilterHard criteria applied to structured MLS fields, never to prose: 34 cleared beds/baths/price, then acreage and pool narrowed it to 8.
VerifyEach survivor is re-read from MetroListPRO, the official MetroList MLS site, and price, beds, full baths, acreage and pool must match. All 8 agreed, checked 2026-08-26.
Cross-enumerateThe three cities were listed again straight from MetroListPRO's own city indexes — 358 active and 63 pending MLS records, a wider net than the IDX feed. Every one of the 284 records the IDX sweep hadn't already covered was pulled and filtered; none of them qualified, so this board is complete against the MLS of record, not just against the feed. Checked 2026-08-26.
ResolveWhere sources disagree, the MLS of record wins and the disagreement is printed on the card rather than hidden.

Two things that bite here and are handled explicitly: half baths — some sites report 2 full + 1 half as “3 baths”, so the bath test counts full baths only; and MetroList MLS number prefixes encode the listing year (226… = 2026), which makes a stale listing easy to spot. Every property on this page carries a 226 number.

Read on the market

The binding constraint is the pool, not the budget. Plenty of 5-bedroom homes on 5+ acres exist in all three towns under $1.5M; very few of them have a pool. Placerville in particular is well stocked with big acreage houses at $1.0–1.2M that have ponds instead of pools — which is why the two strongest all-criteria matches both landed at exactly $1,250,000, and why 5400 Horizon Ct is worth a look despite failing on the pool. Shingle Springs pool properties tend to jump straight from ~$1.5M to $2M+, so 4100 Black Oak Dr at $1.25M is the outlier worth moving on first.

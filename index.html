What the search established

The pool is the binding constraint, not the budget. Homes with 4+ beds on 5+ acres under $1.5M are plentiful across all three towns — this run alone found 20 of them — but only 8 had a pool. Placerville is well stocked with large-acreage homes at $0.6–1.2M that carry ponds, not pools. Pool homes cluster higher: most of this run's matches sit between $1.05M and $1.5M, with a couple of outliers near $0.97M. When a genuine pool property on acreage appears in range, expect it to move — prioritise speed.

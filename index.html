How these were verified

The full active inventory of all three towns — 309 listings — was read from coldwellbankerhomes.com (live MetroList IDX), then every candidate that cleared the bed, bath and price filters was re-checked against MetroListPRO, the official search site of MetroList, which is the MLS for El Dorado County. MetroListPRO exposes a live Status field, so a sold or pending listing cannot read as active.

47 of 48 candidates verified Status: Active. The one that did not is still shown, with the disagreement spelled out on its card rather than resolved silently.

This is what the previous run was missing: it had no network access to any listing site and was inferring status from search-engine snippets, which index sold listings as “For Sale” for years. Acreage and pool data here come from the MLS feature fields, not from listing prose, and every pool was additionally cross-checked against the listing description.

What the market looks like

Now measurable rather than guessed, from 309 active listings. The pool is the binding constraint, not the budget. Of the 48 homes that met 4+ bed, 3+ bath and the $1.5M budget, 20 sit on 2.5+ acres with no pool, while only 6 have both the land and the pool. Dropping the bedroom minimum from 5 to 4 is what made this run productive: it opened up 6 matches where the 5-bed search had found none.

Reading the market

Relaxing the bedroom minimum from 5 to 4 is what opened this search up — 7 of the 9 matches are 4-bedroom, and only 2 would have qualified under the old 5-bedroom rule. The earlier read still holds otherwise: the pool is the binding constraint, not the budget. 14 homes are actively listed that meet everything except the pool, several of them well under $1M on 5–20 acres. Placerville in particular is thick with large-acreage homes at $600K–$1.05M that have ponds and streams rather than pools. If you are willing to add a pool, your options roughly triple and your entry price drops by around $200–300K.

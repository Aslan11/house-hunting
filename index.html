What this run establishes

Of every active single-family listing across the three towns meeting 4+ bd / 3+ ba / under $1,500,000, 19 also clear 2.5 acres — and only 5 of those have a pool. That is the whole search in one line: land and budget are abundant in El Dorado County, pools are not. Placerville carries most of the large-acreage inventory but its $1,000,000–$1,200,000 band is full of houses with ponds rather than pools. Shingle Springs has two of the five. Rescue has exactly one qualifying property in the entire town.

Practically: 3538 Wildwood Ln at $889,000 is the cheapest way into a pool property here and has just moved on price, while 6881 Sagittarius Dr is the one that genuinely satisfies the 5-acre preference at 40 acres. With 5 candidates in a market this thin, none of them is likely to be replaced by something better next month.

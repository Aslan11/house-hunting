What the market looks like

The pool is the binding constraint, not the budget. Across all three towns there is plenty of 4BR/3BA acreage under $1.5M — what's scarce is a pool attached to it. Of every qualifying-size home checked this run, the large majority had no pool, and several of the biggest parcels offered a koi pond, a seasonal stream or a decorative waterfall instead. Three properties on 5–24 acres were ruled out for exactly that reason.

Placerville carries the deepest inventory and the widest price range, and produced half the matches. Rescue is tight — one qualifying property out of the entire active list, with most of its acreage inventory sitting on half-acre to 1.5-acre lots despite the rural address. Shingle Springs clusters its pool properties right at the top of the range, which is why both matches there land between $1.2M and $1.3M.

Timing note: 1988 Cold Springs Rd sold on 4 September 2026 for $840,000 — 4bd/3ba, 2.73 acres, pool. It would have matched every criterion. Properties in this niche clear quickly, and five of the six matches below already carry price reductions, so there is room to negotiate on the ones still standing.
